The problem

The information exists. It's just nowhere to be found when you need it.

Buying a rental property is one of the biggest financial decisions of a lifetime. Yet it is often made on the strength of a listing, a Saturday viewing and a gut feeling.

Dozens of portals

Sale prices, rents, energy ratings, risks, schools, transport: every piece of information lives on a different site, in a different format.

Unreadable raw data

Files with millions of rows, administrative codes, technical maps: everything is public, nothing is readable.

A gut-feeling decision

Short on time, buyers check two or three sources and trust their instinct, on a 20-year commitment.

The solution

One address, twelve ways to read it.

L'Ancura gathers official sources around a property, analyses them geographically and turns everything into readable 0–10 scores, showing what makes a location attractive and what should make you cautious.

Our principles

A decision-support tool, not a crystal ball.

Open data

Only public, official sources. Never scraped listings, never opaque data.

Clarity first

The result should make sense to someone who has never opened a spreadsheet.

Context, not a number

Every score is explained and compared with the area. A 6/10 means nothing on its own.

Honest about uncertainty

When data is missing or weak, the tool says so instead of pretending.

Under the hood

A real data pipeline, built end to end.

FAQ

You're probably wondering…

Is L'Ancura available yet?

L'Ancura is in private beta. Join the waitlist to be among the first users and get the founder rate.

Which areas are covered?

L'Ancura covers France. The data sources are national, and coverage is being extended department by department during the beta: tell us which areas interest you when you sign up.

Where does the data come from?

Exclusively from French public sources under open licences: DGFiP (DVF), INSEE, ADEME, Géorisques, DREES, ANCT and the Ministry of Education, among others.

Does the score replace a viewing or an adviser?

No. L'Ancura informs a decision; it doesn't make it for you. It helps you ask the right questions and compare properties on objective criteria.

How is the score calculated?

Each dimension is scored from 0 to 10 using documented thresholds, then the dimensions are weighted according to the chosen investor profile. When a dimension has no data available, it is flagged and excluded rather than guessed.
